mOSaic

The operating system for organizational AI. Knowledge is a filesystem, agents are processes, and every action is a governed syscall. All on one box.

The problem

Companies want AI that acts on their private knowledge. Today every option breaks one of three things.

It leaves the building

Cloud agents need the finance sheets, tickets and email in someone else's data centre. For regulated teams that ends the conversation.

It acts without asking

An agent that can call tools can call the wrong one. One poisoned email can steer it, and "the model decided to" is not an audit trail.

It forgets where facts came from

Answers arrive without sources, memories never expire, and when a document changes nobody knows which conclusions are now wrong.

Operating systems solved the same problems for programs: isolation, permissions, a kernel that mediates every privileged operation, and a journal. mOSaic applies them to AI agents.

The idea

Take the operating system, and make the programs AI agents.

Governed syscalls

Agent intent is not authorization.

1 · policy

Decide

YAML policy per agent: allow, deny or ask, by capability, path and risk.

2 · approval

Ask a human

The approver sees the arguments, the policy and the cited evidence.

3 · sandbox

Contain

Docker, non-root, read-only, network=none by default.

4 · verify

Check

Read back the world: did the change actually land?

5 · commit

Commit or roll back

A transaction, recorded in the hash-chained journal.

Who it is for · what is next

Teams that can't send their data out, and can't let agents act unchecked.

  • Finance, healthcare, public sector: data residency and approval rules are the law

  • Mid-size companies: one appliance instead of a cloud AI budget

  • Engineering orgs: agents that update trackers and write reports, with a paper trail

Roadmap

  • Appliance: installer and systemd units exist; boot to console on a dedicated box

  • Stronger isolation: microVM sandboxes beside Docker

  • More connectors through MCP: mail, drives, ticketing, chat

  • Identity: SSO, per-team policies and approver roles

  • More than one box: schedule agents across nodes

It doesn't just answer questions. It runs your organization's AI, privately, on one box.
